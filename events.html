Jain Diwali on Capitol Hill

A Light Beyond the Flame: Celebrating Mahavir’s Path to Peace

Mahavir Nirwaan Diwas • A gathering of Jain organizations and community leaders to share the Jain message of ahimsa, compassion and peace on Capitol Hill.

DateTuesday, November 17, 2026
Time6:00 - 8:00 PM
VenueGold Room, Rayburn House Office Building
45 Independence Ave SW
Washington, DC 20515
About the event

A united Jain presence in Washington

Jain Diwali on Capitol Hill is the first major initiative under the Jain American Impact Network.

The event brings together participating Jain organizations to celebrate Mahavir Nirwaan Diwas and introduce Jain values and the Jain community to Congressional leaders and the broader Washington community.

The gathering is intended as a first step toward sustained civic engagement and future Jain-aligned initiatives in America.

Participating organizations

JAINA • JITO USA • JSMW • IDJO • JSNOVA
